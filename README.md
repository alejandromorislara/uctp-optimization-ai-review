# Metodología y Técnicas de Investigación

Material de la asignatura y trabajo sobre optimización e inteligencia artificial aplicadas al *University Course Timetabling Problem* (UCTP), dentro del Máster en Machine Learning y Big Data.

## Estructura

- `Documentacion/`: guía y documentos oficiales de la asignatura.
- `Teoria/`: apuntes, diapositivas y material de consulta.
- `Practicas/`: enunciados, ejercicios y entregables.

La estructura se mantiene deliberadamente plana para que el material sea fácil de localizar.

## Trabajo de investigación

The proposed study reviews exact, metaheuristic, hybrid, and learning-assisted approaches to Curriculum-Based Course Timetabling. Its practical component will reproduce selected experiments from the 2026 open-source LectureKick Simulated Annealing study.

The published solver will be compiled and tested on the 19 non-redundant ITC-2007 UD2 instances. Reproduced objective values and running times will be compared with the reported results, with attention to the execution environment and any discrepancies. ITC-2019 provides context as a newer and richer timetabling formulation.
